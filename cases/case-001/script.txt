A millionaire was found dead in his mansion. His wife, his driver, and his assistant were questioned outside the room. The body was still covered, and police hadn't revealed how he died.

Detective asked, "Does anyone know what happened?"

Nobody answered.

Then the assistant whispered, "He was stabbed in the back."

Wait—what had he just revealed? No one had seen the body. No one had mentioned a knife.

Detective turned to him. "I never said he was stabbed."

The assistant froze. He knew because he was the one who killed him.

Sometimes, the smallest mistake reveals everything.
